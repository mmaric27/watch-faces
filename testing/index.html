How to join

We welcome people who have access to a compatible Wear OS watch and want to genuinely try both apps. You do not need to know the developer personally. If you do not have a compatible watch, you can still read and forward this page without joining or presenting yourself as a tester.

Request Google Group membership

  1. Read the testing privacy notice first. Review the voluntary intention and access terms on this page before sharing information.
  2. Request Google Group membership using the group link below. Use the Google email address you use for Google Play on your watch. Include your watch model; you may also include your Wear OS version. Tell us which apps you are requesting, normally both for this cohort.
  3. Wait for manual owner approval and confirmed access instructions. The group is publicly discoverable and anyone can ask to join. The owner reviews requests. When free access is verified and welcome delivery is enabled, an owner-operated script can send the test and installation links in one private email. The only earlier-send exception is a single-recipient price-check pilot agreed directly with the developer; it receives the same welcome without unconfirmed free-access dates. A request does not guarantee a place or immediate free access.
  4. If you are currently an internal tester, wait for the developer's confirmation. Only after your group membership is approved and the developer confirms the applicable closed-test setup, leave that app's internal test before accepting its closed opt-in. A price-check pilot may check the opt-in and listing without installing; normal installation must wait for confirmed free access. Changing tracks alone does not require reinstallation when the app version and signature are unchanged; contact the developer if your device reports a problem.
  5. Accept both app-specific closed-test opt-ins below. Use the same eligible Google account for group membership, each opt-in and installation. Group membership and publication of a closed release do not themselves opt you in or start your individual 14-day period.
  6. Install onto your watch during the confirmed access window. Use the native Play Store app on your paired Android phone or compatible watch, checking the selected account and watch device. The optional phone installer opens the watch listing using Install or open on watch. The watch face belongs on the watch. After setup, Remove phone installer opens Android's removal confirmation; the watch face stays installed. If Google Play asks for payment at any step, stop and contact the app's support address below; do not pay or use a purchase-and-refund arrangement.
  7. Try the main features and share honest feedback privately. For the actual watch test, aim to keep both apps installed on your watch and remain opted in to both tests for at least 14 consecutive days. Neither needs to remain your active watch face throughout that period. You can leave at any time without penalty.

Use these official links in the order above. The closed opt-ins are for approved members after access instructions are confirmed:

  • First request group membership
  • After approval: Lento closed opt-in
  • After approval: Classic closed opt-in

The voluntary intention we ask you to express is:

I have access to a compatible Wear OS watch and intend to keep both apps installed on the watch and remain opted in to both closed tests for at least 14 consecutive days. I will try the main features and provide honest feedback. Neither face needs to stay active throughout the test. Participation is voluntary, and I can leave at any time.

The continuous opt-in period is separate for each app and Google account. The same people can genuinely test both apps and alternate between the two faces. After trying them, you can use your usual watch face, including if these designs are not to your taste. Keeping both apps installed is our practical test request, not a claim that Google requires either face to stay active. You do not need to wear your watch around the clock or use a face for a fixed number of minutes every day. Removing the optional phone installer does not itself leave the closed test; leaving the Play testing programme or losing group eligibility is a separate action.

Google decides whether production access is granted. Reaching a minimum of 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days is not automatic approval; real testing and the production-access review still matter. We do not claim to have individual session statistics or automated proof of each person's 14 days.

Access for accepted testers

Lento and Lento Classic remain paid apps on Google Play. Any temporary free-installation window must be separately confirmed before participants are invited to install. This page does not announce an active free offer or an approved schedule.

Accepted testers must wait for the developer's confirmation of the applicable installation window. A free-access window is separate from the testing duration; each app still requires its own closed-test opt-in. Previous internal-test access does not guarantee continuing free access.

A separately approved price-check pilot may join both closed tests and inspect the displayed prices before a sale is active. It must not purchase or install either app yet, even if a listing appears free. The developer confirms when installation may begin. A price-check email is not a free offer and does not start our planned watch-use test; Google's account-level opt-in timing remains separate.

Do not purchase an app in order to join this programme or expect a buy-and-refund arrangement. If Google Play asks you to pay, stop and contact Lento support or Lento Classic support for the relevant app before proceeding.

Participation is for testers aged 13 or older who meet the applicable age requirements for their Google account and the apps, using an eligible Google Play account in a country where the relevant app is available and permitted. Leaving the test is voluntary and has no penalty. Continued availability is governed by Google Play's applicable terms and policies, not a promise of permanent availability from the developer.

Testers are not charged for participating. Access is not conditional on a positive public rating or review. See the Google Play Terms of Service.

What to try

  • Initial installation and selecting each app as your watch face.
  • Opening customization and trying the available design settings.
  • Time and date display, including readability in normal use.
  • Always-on display (AOD), if your watch supports it and you choose to enable it.
  • For Lento Classic, the Smooth and Step seconds-hand options, plus Dial marks On/Off and the corresponding numeral/date placement in active mode and AOD.

Tell us what worked, what did not and what you expected. Both positive and negative feedback are useful. These are practical things to explore, not requirements to manufacture activity or report a favourable result. Neither watch face adds in-app telemetry for the test.

Keep feedback private and useful

Use Google Play's private testing feedback option when available, or email the address for the app: Lento support or Lento Classic support. Do not post participant feedback in group conversations. The group is for announcements posted by the owner and read by members.

A short description, your watch model and optional Wear OS version are normally enough to begin. If a screenshot is useful, remove personal details and notification content first. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment details, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information. We do not ask for your date of birth.

To stop participating, leave the relevant Google Play tests and the group. You may also email the developer for help or to request deletion of records the developer controls. Leaving a group is not a substitute for managing each app's Google Play opt-in.

Information handled and why

We handle your application or membership Google name and email address, request message, watch model or Wear OS version you voluntarily provide, which apps you request, test feedback, and welcome-email send-attempt status and timestamps. Access and participation information are handled only to the extent Google makes them available. Truly non-identifying aggregate findings or technical results may be used; removing a name alone is not enough if the remaining details can identify someone.

An owner-operated Google Apps Script, separate from this website and the watch faces, can periodically check group membership to identify eligible approved members and send their private welcome email. The automation does not decide admission, read the support inbox or test feedback, or measure watch-face usage. These records and their accessible version history are subject to the retention and deletion limits below.

Retention and deletion

We retain identifiable test-only records only while needed for a pending application or active participation. We delete them earlier when no longer needed and, in any event, no later than 90 days after the earliest applicable event: withdrawal, rejection, the end of your participation or the end of that cohort. A pending application closes with its cohort if it has not already been decided. Awaiting production approval does not extend this deadline, and remaining in a group for a possible future cohort does not extend retention of old test records. A future cohort requires a new voluntary participation choice.

This ceiling covers test-related received and sent messages, membership and application records, access-administration and email-delivery records, feedback, downloaded attachments, exports and other copies or backups under our control. Deletion is handled by the developer; it is not automated by the watch face or this website.

Genuine separate ongoing ordinary support follows the app policy's ceiling of 12 months after the support request is resolved, with earlier deletion when no longer needed. We do not relabel all testing correspondence as support or retain unrelated test details to extend the testing deadline. Longer retention is limited to information necessary for an applicable legal obligation or a specific legal claim, and only for as long as that reason requires.

You may request earlier deletion using either contact above. We will consider the request and explain any applicable reason preventing immediate deletion. Providers' independent account records, security logs, backups and retention follow their own policies; deleting our controlled copies cannot erase all records independently held by Google or GitHub.
