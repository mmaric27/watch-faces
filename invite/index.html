Interested in testing? See how to request both apps

Request access to both apps

Read the testing privacy notice first. Contact Marko directly with the Google Play email address you want to use. You may include your phone or watch model and optional OS version. This website does not enroll you or collect form data.

Use the Google account selected in the native Play Store app on your Android phone. Add and select that account on the phone if necessary; signing in only in a browser is not enough.

Email Marko about both apps

I manually add your account to both apps' Google Play tester lists before confirming access. When access is ready and the free-installation window is open, you receive one email with everything you need. Use that same account to accept both separate Play opt-ins: Lento and Lento Classic. Then install both apps using the links in your email.

Planned free-access window: 18 October 2026, 00:00 GMT through 31 October 2026, 23:59 GMT, subject to developer confirmation and the actual Play price. Wait for your setup email and check both Play listings before installing. If a price appears, do not pay; contact the developer.

For the test, please aim to keep the companion apps installed and remain opted in to both closed tests for at least 14 consecutive days. This test period is separate from the planned sale window. Try the features available on your actual devices and share honest feedback.

If you have a watch, neither face needs to stay active throughout those 14 days. You can return to your usual face after trying them, including if these designs are not to your taste.

Participation is voluntary and you can leave without penalty. No public rating or review is required.
