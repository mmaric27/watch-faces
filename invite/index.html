An invitation to try two watch faces

Wear OS testers wanted:
Lento & Lento Classic

Hi,

I’m looking for people with a Wear OS 4 or newer watch to help test Lento and Lento Classic, two watch faces I’m developing.

Don’t have a compatible watch?

Please forward this invitation to someone who does, or share it with a relevant community. You don’t need to join the group just to help spread the word.

Share this invitation:

https://mmaric27.github.io/watch-faces/invite/

Lento

One hand. The whole day.
A 24-hour watch face.

Lento Classic

Classic time, thoughtfully composed.
A 12-hour analog watch face.

For the test, please aim to keep both apps installed on your watch and remain opted in to both closed tests for at least 14 consecutive days. Try their features and share honest feedback.

Neither needs to stay your active watch face throughout those 14 days. You can return to your usual face after trying them, including if these designs are not to your taste.

Request membership in the testers’ group:

Request group membership

https://groups.google.com/g/mark-om-watch-faces-testers/about

Use the Google account you use for Play Store on your watch and include your watch model. I approve requests manually. After approval, you’ll receive the applicable setup instructions and test links.

No purchase is required to participate. Wait for confirmed access instructions before installing. If Google Play shows a price, stop rather than paying.

Participation is voluntary. No public rating or review is required.
