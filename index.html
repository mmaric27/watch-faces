Before you buy

A compatible Wear OS 4 or newer watch is required. These apps do not run on Apple Watch or Samsung Tizen watches. Check Google Play for compatibility with your device.

The optional phone companions help open the watch's Play Store listing. They are installers, not watch faces for your phone.

Lento and Lento Classic are sold separately. Prices vary by country and currency. Google Play handles payments; this website has no checkout.

Already a tester? The October 2026 free-installation window has ended. Read the existing tester information and testing privacy notice.

Need a hand?

Email the address for your app. A description of the issue, your watch model and your Wear OS version can help. Share only information needed to explain the problem.

Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, full payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. You do not need to contact support to use either watch face.

The app privacy policies also explain support email handling and the hosting of this website. This site has no checkout, account sign-in or contact form.
