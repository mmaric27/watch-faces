Voluntary testing uses manual Google Play email tester lists for both apps. Contact Marko with the Google account used in the native Play Store on your Android phone, wait for access confirmation, then accept both app-specific Play opt-ins. The website does not enroll you or collect form data.

The planned free-access window is 18 October 2026, 00:00 GMT through 31 October 2026, 23:59 GMT, subject to developer confirmation and the actual Play price. Wait for your setup email and check both Play listings before installing. If a price appears, do not pay.

Read the testing information and privacy notice, or see how to request both apps. Phone-only testing covers the companion interface, instructions and links; watch-face features require a compatible Wear OS watch. Participation and feedback are voluntary, with no public review required and no penalty for leaving.

You are also welcome to browse and forward the public design showcase without becoming a tester.

Need a hand?

Email the address for your app. A description of the issue, your watch model and your Wear OS version can help. Share only information needed to explain the problem.

Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, full payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. You do not need to contact support to use either watch face.

The app privacy policies also explain support email handling and the hosting of this website. This site has no checkout, account sign-in or contact form.
